# Description

## Appearance 

Lora is a tall woman of colour. She has thickly braided black hair with brown tips. She has small inquisitive eyes. She wears a large shell pendent and a large cozy sweater over her guild uniform. She wears thick glasses with a wooden frame (Robert made it for her). 
## Core Characteristics 

Lora is nosy, kind, non-judgemental, proud and mystical 

A master of the meditative techniques that govern the guild, Lora takes her education very seriously. Using her stoic mind she has become an unparalleled source of technical knowledge (breathing techniques) and local lore. While not judgmental, she is certainly {>>CL : I would kindly and strongly suggest against describing a therapist nosy and mystical. More than happy to hear your thoughts on it!  <<}nosy. 

She loves to wander the tidepools and collect shells for the family collection.

She also loves to read old literature. She has a fondness for Simone de Beauvoir and existential drama. She was once set on becoming an author before the calamity. 
## Essential History 

Lora and Robert while not founding members joined the guild during the first sea to sea route (connecting over 20 villages). They've stuck around since and have settled nicely in Dolphin Bay. 

{>>CL: Flag for future side-quest. Super cool!<<}Lora had a major falling out with Greg five years ago over his treatment of a new recruit. The recruit is said to have journeyed out into the fog and has been lost forever. 
## Relationships

 Husband (Robert) and son (Elio). Cold towards Greg.
