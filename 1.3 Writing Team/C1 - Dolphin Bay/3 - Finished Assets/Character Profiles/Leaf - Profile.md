## Appearance 

22 years old. Indian guy. Long wavy black hair. He tends to wear a white crop top, shell necklace and a mermaid themed bathing suit. He's incredibly handsome with a wicked beach bod and the height to match. He has perfectly manicured, well animated eyebrows and an oversized (round) nose that kind of droops. 
## Core Characteristics

He's a surfer dude. He goes with the flow. He isn't very smart, but he's well coordinated and deeply soulful. He likes to read whatever he can find, but he doesn't understand it. He loves to contemplate the simple things like how fish can breath under water (that's a big one). 

He's very friendly and strong, but you have to let him work on his own terms. 

He works for the Dolphin Sanctuary mostly doing heavy lifting and organizational things. Although his passion is working with the animals where he has a natural instinct for the work, and quickly forms strong bonds with the dolphins. Although, he does occasional offer manicuring services and fresh cuts to the more fashionable villagers. 
## Essential History 

Before the calamity, Leaf was traveling the world working as a barber (mostly working in coastal towns). He spent most of the calamity in a coastal town near Dolphin Bay, but he found his way to the bay when he found out about the sanctuary. To him, the job represented a new calling. 

These days he offers surfing lessons to the kids, and is developing a language to communicate with the dolphins. He's pretty convinced that mermaids are real, and has pretty much had confirmation straight from the dolphins. His favorite dolphin is named "Penguin" (not his choice). 
## Relationships

Garp - While Leaf gets along well with the sanctuary workers, he's most attached to Garp. They share a love of the ocean. Garp isn't encouraging of Leaf's surfing, but he secretly admires Leaf's skill. 

Lora - Lora, while not a close friend, will frequently flirt with Leaf. She respects his soulful side. 

Francois - Leaf really looks up to François Hustle. His people skills and braininess are all very impressive. Not to mention, Francois tells a mean story. 

Cat - Leaf doesn't get along with Cat who has no patience for his slow style of teaching. She's a failed surfing and swimming student. He prefers Oliver and kids who know how to chill out.
