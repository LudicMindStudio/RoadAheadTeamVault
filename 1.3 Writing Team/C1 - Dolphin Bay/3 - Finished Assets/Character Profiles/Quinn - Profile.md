# Description

## Appearance

Dark gray eyes, slight eyebags
Sharp facial features. Not very expressive (poker face)
Wears red as a signature color. ex: red jacket over regular gray shirt, black pants, red sneakers
Pale skin and skinny figure
## Core Characteristics 

Quinn is quiet and introverted. They come off as distant and guarded, but are very friendly during interactions. They care a lot about the world and the people around them. They spend most of their time working at Camie’s bakery. Usually behind the counter, taking care of transactions and serving food. They don’t particularly take on any big roles around town and like to keep to themselves. Like a fly on the wall, they observe people coming and going. On slower days they stare out the bakery window, daydreaming and waiting for the time to pass. Outside of work, they hang out at their house. Making music on their dad's old guitar.
## Essential History 

Quinn's original hometown was heavily impacted by the fog. They were sent to Dolphin Bay by their father to keep them safe, while he stayed behind(reasons yet unknown). Due to the calamity and moving towns, Quinn became more guarded.

After getting somewhat acclimated to their new home and having taken a liking to Camie's baked goods and her warm presence, Quinn asked to help out around the bakery. They've been working there ever since.
