# Characters: Lakshmi, Gyo
## Bundle : GyoFeeding_0

Lakshmi has asked about Gyo's research. He lights up — this is clearly his favorite thing to talk about.

Gyo: People think feeding dolphins is just... throwing fish. Pfah! It's an art. It's a science. It's — here, look at this.

*[He holds up two pieces of fish, sliced differently.]*

Gyo: Same fish. Same weight. But this one — see the angle? Diagonal cut. More surface area. The dolphins engage with it differently. They spend more time with it. They play with it before eating. And a dolphin that plays is a dolphin that trusts.

*[He sets them down, almost reverent.]*

Gyo: I've been doing this for over twenty years and I still discover something new every week. Anita says I'm chasing feelings, not data. She's probably right. But the dolphins don't seem to mind.
