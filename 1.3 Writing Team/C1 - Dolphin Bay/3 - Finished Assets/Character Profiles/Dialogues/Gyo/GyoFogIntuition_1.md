# Characters: Lakshmi, Gyo
## Bundle : GyoFogIntuition_1

*[Gyo watches the dolphin circle. One lap. Two.]*

Gyo: Not exactly. I think they feel something we can't measure. The fog isn't just weather. It changes how things feel. The light is different. The air is heavier. The animals notice before we do.

*[He rests his forearms on the railing.]*

Gyo: Pigs get restless before fog comes in. Geese stop squabbling. And the dolphins... the ones that left, they left before the fog got bad. Like they knew it was coming and didn't want to be here for it.

*[He glances sideways at Lakshmi.]*

Gyo: That's not science. That's just me paying attention for twenty-five years. But I've learned to trust what I see.
