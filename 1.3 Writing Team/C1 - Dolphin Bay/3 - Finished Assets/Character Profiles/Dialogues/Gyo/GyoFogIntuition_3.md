# Characters: Lakshmi, Gyo
## Bundle : GyoFogIntuition_3

*[Gyo turns to look at Lakshmi. Something warm and surprised flickers across his face.]*

Gyo: Does it? I was afraid I was just getting old. You know, when you've been in one place long enough, you start seeing patterns that aren't there. Apophenia. That's the word Anita would use.

*[He returns his gaze to the dolphin pen.]*

Gyo: But it doesn't feel like a false pattern. It feels like the kind of truth you can only find by living someplace for decades. Knowing the way the light falls at 4 PM in winter. Knowing which geese are paired and which are loners. Knowing that something changed before you had the language to describe it.

*[He smiles, small and genuine.]*

Gyo: So thank you for not telling me it's nonsense. I needed someone to hear that without correcting it.
