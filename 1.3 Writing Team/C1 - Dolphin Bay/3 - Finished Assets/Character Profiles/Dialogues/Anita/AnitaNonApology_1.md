## Bundle : AnitaNonApology_1

*[Anita shakes her head. Not at Lakshmi. At the thought.]*

Anita: Trying. I keep hearing Gyo say that word whenever I fail at something. "You were trying." As if intention and outcome occupy the same moral category.

*[She taps the metronome, resetting its rhythm.]*

Anita: I gave him terms and conditions when he needed a person. I didn't know how to be a person in that moment. I still don't know if I could do it differently. That's the part I can't reconcile.

*[The metronome ticks.]*

Anita: I don't want credit for trying. I want to be able to do it right next time.
