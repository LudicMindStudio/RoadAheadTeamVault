## General Description 

Lakshmi sits at the desk and does her theoretical test.

{++### The written exam

Three questions, asked at the desk. The player can get them wrong. The exam moves on either way, and Greg's read-out afterwards stays gentle.

Each question offers a **Recall** option (Lakshmi's memory supplies the hint) and a **Check notes** option. Wrong answers get their own short musings from Lakshmi.

**Question 1.** The Fog was a Calamity that took many lives. The fog is most receptive to which human condition?
- Hunger: "It was hunger that makes the fog appear, right?"
- A raised voice: "Don't talk in the fog, maybe?"
- Emotion: "Emotions. The fog is dangerous to everyone. But most of all to a mind that's struggling."
Recall: "I remember covering this in class. Mental health. Emotions. The fog is dangerous to everyone, but most of all to a mind that's struggling."

**Question 2.** The Postal Guild keeps offices in over one hundred locations. What is its purpose?
- Fortune: "Fortune. The guild tries to make money in the new world."
- Adventure: "Every guild member wants a good adventure!"
- Connection: "Communication. Delivering mail. The guild keeps people connected, and all of us are stronger for it."
Recall: "Ah, over a hundred offices. A safe way to reach each other, ever since the fog."

**Question 3.** We are the survivors who rebuilt everything. Including how we treat each other. What did we build the new world on?
- The developed laws of the past: "The old laws, maybe? I don't remember seeing any courts around."
- A crown: "Was it monarchy? I don't remember seeing any kings around."
- Community: "Community. Our neighbours and the guild support each other. We're all in this together."
Recall: "Our textbook covers how every other form of government fell apart. Somehow, the fog incentivizes communities."

Then the paper goes back to Greg for review.

**Asides.** The exam paper is the future minigame surface. Its side dialogue does not use the main dialogue renderer: if the player circles nothing, a quiet line from Lakshmi nudges them to circle an answer. If the player traces the little shapes on the paper, she speaks to herself, and the insight lands.++}
