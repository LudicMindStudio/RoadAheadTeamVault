# Quest Description

## Root Act

Dolphin Bay Village Story (Act 2)

## General Description — "A Delivery Disrupted"

Lakshmi investigates the sanctuary for herself. Gordon mentions that fish deliveries to the sanctuary have slowed — Francois's protest has a quiet, petty arm. This is Lakshmi's entry point to the sanctuary, her first meeting with Anita and Gyo, and the first crack in the "sanctuary bad" narrative.

The quest reveals that Francois isn't a villain — he's hurting and lashing out. He could have cut the supply entirely. Instead he's doing something small and petty. A vent, not a strike. This humanizes him.
