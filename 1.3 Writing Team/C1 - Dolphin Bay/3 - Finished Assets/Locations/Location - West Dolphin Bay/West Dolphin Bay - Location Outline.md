# Location Description

## Purpose

{++The west residential half. First town texture, Cat's home turf, and the beach layer.++}

## Vibe and visual description

{++Bright and colorful; thin wooden home walls, plenty of windows; stone paving with grass growing through; semi-tropical. Waves and a constant breeze; wildlife about. The beach layer hugs the water; the dwellings sit up in the forest, not on the sand. Street lights on the main streets; the landscape cleared and structured.++}

## Outdoor Character

{++- The Coastal Promenade: below the area heading west toward Outer Dolphin Bay, a serene worn-dirt path descending all the way to the beach side, lined with trees and flowers. Quaint until the stony area where it naturally emerges into sand. A faster way down: the stairs by the wall. Birds and squirrels, seen and heard.
- The Beach: morning traps and oyster harvest with Garp; seaweed washed up along the shore.
- The Gazebo Garden: Doran & Shirley's, next to their house; a little bird feeder, birds stop by.
- The Greenhouse: Shirley's pride, filled with exotic finds from her travels; she works it most afternoons. See [[The Greenhouse - POI]].
- The way down to Garp's place on the water by the dock: reached mainly from a path on the north-eastern side; also accessible from the shore.++}
