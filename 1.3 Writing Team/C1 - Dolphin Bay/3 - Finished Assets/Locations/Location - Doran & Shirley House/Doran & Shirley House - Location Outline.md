# Location Description

## Purpose

Permanent home of Doran and Shirley. Restored by Doran after it was abandoned during the calamity. 

## Vibe and visual description

Holy wow it's colorful! Think of the happiest, brightest expression of latinx culutre and match that with years of delicate care and restoration from a gifted craftsman. Shirley has covered the place in colorful pots with fun plants. They are everywhere! Doran and Shirley have built themselves a little paradise. 

It's a cozy two floor building. The second floor is smaller allowing the upstairs bedroom to open to a large balcony lit by fairy lights. Surrounding the home is a beautiful covered porch with a tile floor. A tiled path leads from the porch to a gazebo which is spacious enough for the locals to meet and dance. On the opposite side of the property, Doran has built his little workshop. The outside is painted bright red. He has various sawhorses  

Large luminous windows. Cozy tapestries and bright throw blankets. A substantial kitchen. You'll want to stay for awhile.
