## Zones (Indoor / Outdoor)

### Outdoor Zone
- Follows Dolphin's Bay aesthetic but the focus is on plants and flowers. 
- There's a small garden in the front and a bigger one in the back. In the back, you can also find the beehives where she collects honey. Vines with colorful flowers can be seen on the outer walls of the house.
- Part of the garden uses old materials such as a bathtub to serve as a flower pot. A couple of big water containers are on the side of the house to collect rain water.
- Flowers are being grown but we can also find carrots, tomatoes, basil, thyme, mint and other things that are eatable. 
- Lisa uses flowers and different herbs to infuse her teas. 
### Indoor Zone - Floor By Floor Layout
- One floor house. Lisa is old and doesn't want to go up some stairs all the time. 
- We can find an entryway, a living room, a dinning room, a kitchen, a bathroom and a closed bedroom.
- In the living room stands an old but nice piano. Around it,  you can find some comfortable seating.
- The dinning room is where Lisa receives her guests. There a table that can accomodate 4 people. 
- The kitchen is rather small but has all the essentials.
- All around the house, there is a few house plants, but a lot less than outside. Lisa likes to be outdoor to take care of her plants.
