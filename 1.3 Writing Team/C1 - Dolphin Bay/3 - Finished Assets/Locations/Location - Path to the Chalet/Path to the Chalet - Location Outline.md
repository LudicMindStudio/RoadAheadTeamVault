# Location Description

## Purpose

{++The forest path, the Walk. The descent from the chalet down to the town's west edge; the movement showcase (mantling).++}

## Vibe and visual description

{++Hardy, hilly forest; sparse enough to run through, clearings forming the path; real height bands with ledges. Glimpses of the bay through the trees. The eastern side keeps the shore front, like the chalet. (Kids say monsters lurk here.)++}

## Outdoor Character

{++- Nature, wildlife, pathways. Barren until the town; the descent carries small wildlife events.
- The path itself: one continuous route with no loading break, falling through height bands (h0 to h2 to h6 to h14) over mantling ledges, with a vista or two before the first full view of the bay.
- The other bench: Grandpa Dan's, facing the water.
- Grandpa Dan's house sits off the path, in the woods: a small cottage with a bedroom, a kitchen, one small room with two chairs and a fireplace, and the clutter room. Below it, further down the cliffside: a little fire pit and a spot on the beach, looking out at the fog on the water.++}
