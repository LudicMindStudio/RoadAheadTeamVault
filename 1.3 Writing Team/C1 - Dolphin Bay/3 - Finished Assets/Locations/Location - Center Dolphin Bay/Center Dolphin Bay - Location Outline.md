# Location Description

## Purpose

{++The town square and the Guild Hall. The arrival, and the hub.++}

## Vibe and visual description

{++The tail-end where the bay meets the ocean. The water splits the town, and a small island with bridges connects the halves. The Guild Hall stands on the bisecting island, the centrepiece of the town. The sea wall runs along the southern side of the maps (Central, West, and East): a big, natural-looking wall of massive stones pushed up along the cliffside facing the water, with flat shore beaches at its foot. At high water it smashes against the wall.++}

## Outdoor Character

{++- The Bridges: sturdy rope, solid. Half of each bridge sits in Center, the other half in the West and East maps (that's where the scene transitions go). People stop on them to watch the water.
- Docks along the central island's back side: guild-used, currently out of service.++}
