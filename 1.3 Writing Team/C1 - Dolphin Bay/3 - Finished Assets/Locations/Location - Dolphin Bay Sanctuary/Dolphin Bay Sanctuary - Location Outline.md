# Location Description

## Root Village

[[Dolphin Bay - Outline]]

## Visual Description

A weathered barn-house perched at the edge of the water. The building itself is old, pre-Calamity, maybe, with faded paint and a tin roof that catches the light. Out front, a short pier extends into the bay with a couple of small boats tied to it. Wooden buoys bob at the dock, painted in faded blues and whites. Along the pier, dolphin pens sit mostly empty, mesh enclosures in the water large enough to hold a pod, now holding only one or two.

To the side of the main building, secondary pens hold a small collection of other animals, pigs, dogs. A survey tree on the edge of the property has equipment strapped to it: a camera, some kind of sensor.

The signage is old and weather-worn. A hand-painted sign reads "Dolphin Bay Research Sanctuary."

## General Description

The Dolphin Bay Research Sanctuary is the epicenter of the village's central conflict. On the surface: a facility dedicated to studying and caring for dolphins, with secondary research on fog-resilient animals. In practice: two aging researchers who are better at communicating with dolphins than with people, a protest that misunderstands everything they do, and a piece of monitoring equipment in the lab that Dr. Lamborghini provided, connected to something larger that neither researcher fully understands.

The sanctuary feels lived-in. Cluttered, but functional. Equipment stacked in corners. Coffee cups in multiple states of use. The hum of Anita's audio rig in one room. The smell of fish from Gyo's kitchen. A workspace that two people have occupied for decades and personalized completely.

# Quest-Specific Changes

Per quest, the sanctuary may gain temporary interactables, characters, or altered states:

### During Quest A (The Missing Fish)
- Fish supplies visibly low. Gyo's workstation sparse.
- Gyo may mention rationing.

### During Quest B (The Secret Research)
- The fog-monitoring equipment in the lab becomes more prominent.
- A notebook or data log may be present as a temporary interactable.

### During Quest D (The Wrong Frequency)
- Anita's audio setup is more active. Extra equipment in the lab.
- Anita may be absent from her usual positions.

### Post-Dolphin Bay story
- Tone is lighter. Coffee cups are clean. Dolphins begin to return to the pens.
- Both Anita and Gyo are more relaxed.
