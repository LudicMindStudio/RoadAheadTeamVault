[[1. Dolphin Bay Npc Profiles]]
# Content
A fully fledged jaded member of dolphin bay. Stuck reminiscing and pessimistic about the future compared to the past. Sounds wise but really is just using his age to reinforce his belief

You can find him checking his lobster cages by the warf. He doesn't find much and it doesn't surprise him. 
# Appearance

He's tall but bent over and gaunt. He wears an extra large black jacket that covers him completely, and nuzzles his cheek. His pocket has a big x stitched into it to keep it together (clearly done by a loving hand). He wears a trapper hat.
