# Content
Camie is the local baker. They approach their craft with much artistry, and are inspired by aquatic life to create new experimental loaves (like a sea-weed loaf). Camie is also deeply sensitive to the dolphin cause, and concerned that the organization of their society is perpetuating ongoining issues. 

Fed by the misinformation of Francois, Camie has totally lost touch with reality, and they are ready to follow the activists into dangerous waters.
