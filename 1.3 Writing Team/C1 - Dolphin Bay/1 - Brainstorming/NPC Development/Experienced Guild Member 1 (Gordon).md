# Content
Tries to help Lakshmi. Energetic. Quick tips guy

His lively energy makes him a good friend of Garp the sailor. They love to exchange stories of their various adventures into the fog with much drama! They met out of convenience with the guild members often relying Garp's shipping. 

These days Gordon is mostly in charge of guild logistics, such as, planning out larger deliveries and attempting to establish connection with new communities.
