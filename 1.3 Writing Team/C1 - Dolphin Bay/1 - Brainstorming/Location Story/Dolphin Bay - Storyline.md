# Dolphin Bay - Storyline

What is the storyline of the game going to be?
	- This is where the game begins
	- This is where Lakshmi takes her test to become a postal worker
	- Ideally, we would want to have a storyline which would flow into the other villages and be a trigger point for the entire game

What are interesting NPCs that we can build upon to create a story within Dolphin Bay
	- **The captain sailor**
		- Name: **Captain Garp**
		- Mysterious at first, he wants to sail again but acknowledges the difficult currents and a lack of a crew as the reasons behind being unable to do it so far
		- Lakshmi and him could have a common interest in that they both want to explore the world
			- This fits with her role as a postal worker, she will join his crew to move to the next village
	- **The four young people on the south east coast of Dolphin Bay**
		- Names:
			- Girl on the left: **Camie**
			- Guy in the middle: **Doran**
			- Guy on the right: **Gatz**
			- Guy facing all three of them: **Francois Hustle**
	- **The Grandma**
		- Name: **Marianne**
		- She invites Lakshmi for tea. This could be an event that is unlocked once she becomes a relevant character in the plot
	- **The intern**
		- Name: **Olavius**
