# Main Story Outline:

The world has been struck by [[Writer's Index#Calamity|Calamity]]. Out of nowhere, a grey [[Writer's Index#Fog|Fog]] spread across the land, blotting out the sun. People all over the globe looked in terror as their world transformed into a colorless landscape. They didn't know what to expect, but shortly after its first appearance the dark fog began to cause an [[Writer's Index#Fog illness Greying|illness]]. The ones exposed were met with a terrible fever and cough. This strange phenomenon accelerated the spread of the [[Writer's Index#Fog|fog]] and, very soon, no city was left untouched. Many people perished from the [[Writer's Index#Fog illness Greying|illness]]. Governments broke, communities fractured, families destroyed. Social order fell apart and society crumbled. ==*Fifteen years*== have passed. Survivors have since coalesced into smaller communities throughout the world, banding together to create their own little worlds of comfort. [[Writer's Index#Lakshmi|Lakshmi]], the game protagonist, is a [[#Postal Guild (aka. Guild Hall)|postal carrier]], strong, confident, and optimistic, but still working toward recovery and acceptance after losing her family in the calamity. [[Writer's Index#Villages|Villages]] portray different mental health journeys and [[Writer's Index#Coping Strategy (aka coping mechanism)|coping strategies]], making the game environment/levels.

# Lore Timeline
### Pre-Calamity 
The world as we know it today.

### Calamity
The event sending the world into irreversible change.

The world has been struck by [[Writer's Index#Calamity|calamity]]. Out of nowhere, a grey [[Writer's Index#Fog|fog]] spread across the land. Blotting out the sun, people all over the globe looked in terror as their world transformed into a colorless landscape. 

The people didn't know what to expect, but shortly after its first appearance the dark [[Writer's Index#Fog|fog]] began to cause an [[Writer's Index#Fog illness Greying|illness]], later known as Greying. The people exposed were met with a terrible fever and cough. When the ill would cough, more of the fog would come bursting out of their lungs. This strange phenomenon accelerated the spread of the [[Writer's Index#Fog|fog]] and very soon, no city was left untouched. 

*Note: The calamity is one event, one moment in time. Unlike the post calamity and pre-calamity, we can plan around a small series of events and then add more and more specifics as our story grows when we need them. Here is a skeleton we can build off of:*  

### Post-Calamity:
The world has gone through disaster. Typical social order has fallen apart and many people have died. The people who survived decided to band together and create small communities. Though much of the landscape is barren, most of these communities have managed to achieve some amount of comfort by forming [[Writer's Index#Villages|villages]]. The state of technology in this world is about the same as the real world, but perhaps some goods are more scarce and a lot of technology has a sort of patchwork quality to it.

In the dawn of the new world, a collection of passionate *postal carriers* banded together. They believed that even in a devastated world, the need for postal service is vital. Even more so, given the failure of all forms of telecommunications. Together, they created a network of mail service [[Writer's Index#Postal Guild (aka. Guild Hall)|guilds]] throughout the world to help deliver letters and packages for the people. As the years passed, some passionate fellows decided to establish a *[[Writer's Index#Postal Guild (aka. Guild Hall)|guilds]]* of *postal carriers*. They believe in the importance of having a working mail system as they believe it helps to bring humanity close together (in the new world, most communications technologies, like cell phones and internet, probably wouldn't work very well or not at all). As such, this guild has worked to create numerous chapters around the world. They coordinate with each other to create a world wide network. The [[Writer's Index#Postal Guild (aka. Guild Hall)|postal guild]] was established by people who were *postal carriers* in the old world before the disaster. Most of the older workers are still active (as it has only been *==fifteen years==*) and they generally serve in higher positions. Most chapter leaders are older people who helped to establish the guild.

Due to the nature of this new world, *postal carriers* are expected to be a bit more hardy than they used to be. Because they may travel between towns/cities and because so much of the wilderness has taken over, they need to be equipped with certain survival skills.

There should be *transitional spaces* between villages. Those places should be especially dangerous. [[Writer's Index#Fog events (aka. Fog creatures and Fog puzzles)|Fog events]] would spawn in these places.

# Game Plots

### Main Plots
*Note: These two main plots should connect at a point (climax) of the story.*
##### Lakshmi’s trauma subplot
[[Writer's Index#Lakshmi|Lakshmi]] lost her parents when the fog appeared but she does not remember it. She is very motivated to learn more of the mystery. As we explore the mystery more (as writers) [[Writer's Index#Lakshmi|Lakshmi]] will in some way be related to the [[Writer's Index#Fog|fog]], and is cruxed by how she chooses to cope with it.
##### The fog subplot
How and why the [[Writer's Index#Fog|fog]] appeared. 
### Location Specific Plots 
##### Village subplot
Self-contained stories that affect the [[Writer's Index#Villagers|villagers]] within, and, over game progression, intervillage relations. Locations progress until they are in a final state, where the [[Writer's Index#Villages|villages]] is then at normal or “ideal” operation - when the story has concluded and things are better.
##### Social connectedness subplot
All [[Writer's Index#Villages|villages]] are socially isolated. As game progresses, the fog lifts and changes people’s lives. Our [[Writer's Index#Lakshmi|main character]] is in her early twenties and has recently finished her training to become a postal carrier. She is very eager and enthusiastic about her job and very much looks forward to all of the new people she is going to meet. [[Writer's Index#Lakshmi|Lakshmi]]’s eagerness is used to connect people. By simply talking and doing basic “Quests” people are brought together and connected, where they otherwise wouldn't be. Progress can be seen by the change in habituation of the NPCs Lakshmi effects. 
##### Memories subplot
Even smaller self-contained stories affecting only Lakshmi’s journal entries. They have little effect on the outside world other then the specific villager whose memory it was. 
###### Memory entering abilities subplot
Lakshmi gained the ability to enter memories but does not know how to control them at first. 

*Note that crossing over these different plots together is what will make the world feel alive and connected. At some point completing a memory might be needed for an NPC to change and do something to push forward the main plot. Or the completion of the Village plot revealing some secret in the main plot. We can start by designing them in a vaccum but look for ways to interconnect our world.*

### 1) Postal Guild Graduation 
Lakshmi starts her journey in Dolphin Bay, the first village, where she graduates the postal guild and learns her first coping strategy (assertiveness). 

- *Start [[#Social connectedness subplot]]:* After having done this a few times, she may recognize a pattern within the memories. Perhaps they all have to do with a sense of loneliness or some kind of mental health struggle. She now feels like her job is not just about postal service, but something deeper. She realizes she can truly help people with her abilities.
- Later in the game, she discovers the fog to be a manifestation of the emotional isolation people were already feeling, her previous doubts confirmed. Everyone has unique stories, but the feeling of isolation is shared. The more she helps individuals, the more the fog lifts. And although individual struggle and trauma do not magically disappear, people can face them better in community.

- *Start [[#Memories subplot]]:* Individual memories show clues about Lakshmi’s parents and she learns that some of the people she helps were connected to them (see Dr. Lamborghini plot). She may feel eager to find more clues to learn about her own origins. Greg will remind her that while she is curious about her past, it is also important not to lose sight of her present mission. He tells her that digging into her past may not bring her the satisfaction she seeks, and sometimes helping others can alleviate one’s own challenges. So she continues her job.
- Some people she helps end up helping her in return by pointing her in the direction of her parents (I’m thinking an NPC recognizes her because she looks like her parents, and gives her insights). As she gathers more info, she discovers her parents were doing research with Dr Lamborghini (Filip’s character) to stop the fog, during the beginning stages of it. But they didn’t approach it the right way, and the fog intensified, leading to their passing. (This could be why Dr Lamborghini was then trying to erase people’s memories. Thinking it could make amends.) (the point here is to highlight how even good intentions don’t always lead to smart actions, and how Lakshmi being the one who can help lift the fog is like a karmic thing)

# More Detailed Narrative Guidelines
*Notes: (not the same as technical formatting to make assets, used for implementation)*

#### Dialogue General Writing Guidelines
[[Writer's Index#Villagers|Villagers]] are dynamic; they can change and grow with the world. What the [[Writer's Index#Villagers|Villagers]] have to say given their individual circumstances changes as the player plays the game. A story event made a protest, that blocks this villagers morning walk? Their walk path will have to change until the protest is gone and they might have more dialogue to grumble about it. The caricatures that we put into even our most basic of villagers should be able to change in some way. 

As simple as: Lakshmi visits new villagers for first time -> “thanks for the mail oh wow its from X, i was waiting for this!” -> second time -> “oh wow another package, i wonder if its from X again” 
#### Main Character (Lakshmi) General Writing Guidelines
[[Writer's Index#Lakshmi|Lakshmi]] will need to have mannerisms and traits that manifest, no matter how much control we want to give the player. Obviously, the story and events she experiences will be largely the concern of her dialogue, but what type of person will she be? We need to have [[Writer's Index#Lakshmi|Lakshmi]] portrait some emotions for the players to actually reflect on, no matter how much of a slate she is. In game, in a traditional rpg sense, this can manifest as choosing between the *Playful dialogue*, or the *Brave dialogue*, or the *Curious dialogue* etc.. We could escalate this all the way up and think about a game design approach involving stats and more. Or, as the complete opposite consideration, we can just have simple but relatable emotions, given each scenario. Think more Link from Zelda, he embodies courageous and positivity always. The events around Link are awe-full and he reacts to make his emotions clear. For [[Writer's Index#Lakshmi|Lakshmi]] she would embody empathy and make these choices easily, without player interaction. 

These are loose ideas but both can be true depending on the mechanical type of narrative we are telling (side quest -> always empathetic, main quest -> personality traits surface) 

#### The Fog General Writing Guidelines

In a design way, the [[Writer's Index#Fog|fog]] is the manifestation of negative mental health. Defining negative mental health is the fun part. We get to carefully and respectfully craft stories and then portrait the actions in it as influencing the [[Writer's Index#Fog|fog]], influenced by negative mental health. 

This can be seen in the [[#2) Dolphin Bay Village Story|Dolphin Bay Story]] with the protests as the recursively forming the plot thread. The negative mental health of the mob (bandwagonning, hostile) causes more fog to manifest, which fuels the mob. Narrative ties between the fog and the people who exist around it can get as elaborate as we wish. The [[Writer's Index#Fog|fog]] that is a shadow.

In becoming a reflection of our characters it can become whatever we want of it, narratively and mechanically. This would allow for larger manifestations of antagonistic properties from the [[Writer's Index#Fog|fog]] to develop into vague abstractions of characters, or at least play narrative beats.

The physical storytelling of the fog will be highly tethered to [[#Lakshmi’s trauma subplot|Lakshmi's Backstory]] but as it is now, the [[Writer's Index#Fog|fog]] does not follow physical rules, it's a magic magufin. [[Writer's Index#Fog|Fog]] exists everywhere across the land, except for places it doesn't. If it doesn't exist somewhere it should have a reason, but otherwise the [[Writer's Index#Fog|fog]] can take any random form at any random location. Fog creatures, [[Writer's Index#Fog events (aka. Fog creatures and Fog puzzles)|Fog events]], and Fog puzzles can all be given form without some connected story. All still connected to the themes of the fog, but not so connected to time/place.

Regardless, the [[Writer's Index#Fog|fog]] should be a heavy and interesting theme with obvious direction, [[Writer's Index#Fog|fog]] bad. we can manifest that basic as is, or add depth tending towards our goals for the project.
