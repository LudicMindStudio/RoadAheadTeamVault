[[Narrative Goals#The fog mystery|Fog Mystery plot]]

The story of the Calamity begins with Lakshmi’s parents, two scientists(?) who are researching ways to make the world better. Their research is highly connected to people's emotions, and involves studies with people of varying different mental health states.

We then need to connect the story to the fog. some miracle gas that served as a medicine through an inhaler? the manifestation of some power/energy that allowed for treatment along with some proprietary technology? Lakshmi is in some way exposed to this "source" of the research and it leads into her gaining her powers.

Some accident occurs (can be connected to Lakshmi or not) to this promising new research on the day it's to be revealed and shared with the world at large. A silent catastrophe that the world didn't know hit them. Lakshmi's parents, fearful of the accident, try to bring people together, try to connect with the people around them. Lakshmi's parents burn everything out trying to solve the mess they've made but they fail. The fog rolls in without anyone giving it mind, before the illnesses begin and its all too late.
