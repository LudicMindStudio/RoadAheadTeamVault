Old Profile: 
Grandpa Dan is eccentric, impulsive and can be a little reckless. He is the tank of the team. He is loud and short-tempered/grumpy. He **fondly remembers the time before the fog hit** and is now lonely and just wants some friends and a little adventure. He lost his family in the fog, which he thinks includes his daughter. However, they reconnect in the last village while delivering a package to her. At first they don't recognize each other. They start yelling/bickering at each other and then he realizes she's his daughter and he starts crying and hugs her. He wants to finish the final quest since he's come this far and agrees to move back to that village to live near his daughter once the quest is over.

  
He used to be a postal worker and only brings it up late in the game and he also has a dog, Leo. TBD in fights, he can call on Leo to attack or pet him and squeeze his cheeks to lower stress levels. Leo cannot be hurt.
