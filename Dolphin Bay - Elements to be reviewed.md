# Dolphin Bay - Elements to be reviewed

When Lakhsmi does her quiz to become a postal worker, I think the questions we are asking the player may have an evident answer to us, but it might be difficult for the player to know the answer to these questions, since he doesn't know much about the setting and the lore of the game
	- Should we opt for different questions or have more explanation prior to the player reaching that part of the game?

The conversation with the intern doesn't make much sense
	- What is the flashback? Is it a flashback? Maybe it is meant to be an introduction into the theme of self-confidence
	- If they are messing everything up, why does Lakshmi tell them she learned a lot from them?
	- My proposal is the intern can be an important character of the Dolphin Bay arc, but...
		- We shouldn't introduce their low self-belief so soon
		- We can show, in this scene, that they aren't great at their job
		- We should not convey the message that someone with low self-belief should be fooled into thinking they are actually good and inspiring
			- Rather, Lakshmi seemingly the more natural and adept mailperson, should take him up as her companion once she's done a few mail deliveries in the village and feels confident teaching the intern how to go about things
		- Simultaneously, the intern can open up about their low self-confidence, and that being one more reason why Lakshmi feels the need to help them

Getting to Bubble Town is too easy and leaving Dolphin Bay so early makes it a missed opportunity to build the plot and the village we've designed
	-The first few mail deliveries should be to townspeople
		- This can serve as a tutorial of the gameplay mechanics as a mailperson
	- Maybe the first two can be pretty basic. Just some dialogue that adds some life into the NPCs of our game
	- Then, the third one can be more complex, the person who's mail we're supposed to deliver has disappeared
		- Greg tells us to leave it, as we are mail people, not detective, but the youthful Lakshmi seeks to find him, both because she's worried about him, and because she wants to deliver the mail
	- The mystery of his disappearance can be related to the secret of the dolphins and to the Fog
	- As such, the plot within Dolphin Bay can be to with the goal of finding our disappeared man
