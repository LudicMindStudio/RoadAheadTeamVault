# Agenda

---
We are going to cover scene and script writing. Trent will show some of the scenes writing for Act1 of dolphin bay and explain a bit the process. Then the team will work together to do a live synopsis for one of the next scenes. 

We will then briefly go into script writing. The idea is to think of both dialogue lines and physical reactions. Write actions into the script and then it becomes a later question of how to actually implement them. they can be ignored in the future or clever solutions can arise for how to enact on them in game.

Finally, ask the team what they think of the approach to writing and get a sense of if things feel like they are connecting. Summarize the different sub-sub domains of work here within the writing team. Plot writing, Scene writing, Dialogue writing. 

As homework the team members should try to do each one of these at least once. If they like one more then the other then we will encourage and assign tasks accordingly in the future.
