# Context
Happens after the intro cut scene that gives the overall context of the game.

# Characters
- young Lakshmi
- Lashmi's dad

# Content
**Intro**
	Soft fade in. Sound of a creaking swing and soft breeze
**Young Lakshmi**
	_...and then I'll visit Bubble Town and be the best postal worker ever!_
**Lakshmi's Dad**
	_Haha yes I'm sure you will be Lakshmi, the best there ever will be! Lakshmi, come here for a moment. I have something to show you._
**Player Event**
	Use the arrow keys to move. Space to interact. Move to the highlighted square and interact.
**Event**
	Lashmi comes closer to her dad to interact with him. 
**Young Lakshmi**
	_What do you have there Dad?_
**Lakshmi's Dad**
	_This is a little grasshopper Lakshmi. It was jumping around out here in the forest._
**Young Lakshmi**
	_Wow so cute!_
**Lakshmi's Dad**
	_Lakshmi, this bug may be little but it is a precious creature with a life just like you and I. It has desires and wants, just like us, and knows how to live its life as a grasshopper._
**Young Lakshmi**
	_(Love bubble)_
**Lakshmi's Dad**
	_...What do you think the grasshopper is thinking about Lakshmi?_
**Young Lakshmi**
	_(...). Hmmm... I don't know Dad, maybe hopping?_
**Lakshmi's Dad**
	_Think closely Lakshmi. This grasshopper has lived a life different from yours and experienced different things. Put yourself into the place of the grasshopper._
**Event**
	Lashmi faces the player. She's confused and sad. The screen flashes. 
**Young Lakshmi**
	_... I think...he's scared._	
**Lakshmi's Dad**
	_(!) Hahaha, very good Lakshmi. You would be scared too if two giants were standing over you no?. Let's leave the little grasshopper alone and go get some lunch._
**Young Lakshmi**
	_Lunchtime! Yeaaaa! Comeon Dad!_
**Event**
	Lashmi leaves the screen.	
**Lakshmi's Dad**
	_Ho there! Slow down little lady!._
**Event**
	Screen fades out with Lakshmi's dad leaving.
