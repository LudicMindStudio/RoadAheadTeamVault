--Main character

Name: Lakshmi

Age: 23

Occupation: Fresh recruit at the postal carrier guild

Biography:

She was a young girl when the disaster happened and does not have much memory of it. She was orphaned as a result of the fog. Gregory took her in and raised her as his own daughter. Despite her own tragic past she maintains a very positive outlook on life. An unrepentant pollyanna, she is quick to find positivity in life. She looks forward to starting her work as a postal carrier and is eager to meet new people and see new places.
  

--Guild leader

Name: Greg (Gregory)

Age: 59

Occupation: Leader of the chapter where the main character is a postal carrier

Biography:

Worked as a simple postal man before the disaster. He helped to build the postal carrier guild and was one of the original founders. He has a militaristic style to things, but this is mostly a façade. He is very kind and forgiving and looks after new recruits. He is very passionate about mail delivery. Even before the disaster he had plenty of experience in this profession. He tends to goof off from time to time and his deputy has to keep him in line.


--Deputy

Name:

Age: 32

Occupation: Deputy Leader of the postal carrier chapter

Biography:

She was almost an adult when the disaster struck. She was quite fortunate in that neither her parents, nor her siblings perished in the disaster. Looking for a sense of purpose, she decided to join Gregory in his mission. Gregory took her under his wing as a protégé. She learned under him and became his right hand. She is far more serious than Gregory and more organized. She keeps an eye out on Gregory and ensures that he doesn't go too off the rails.
