The world has been struck by calamity. As if from out of nowhere, a grey fog spread across the land. Blotting out the sun, people all over the globe looked in terror as their world transformed into a colorless landscape.

The people didn't know what to expect, but shortly after its first appearance the dark fog began to cause an illness. The ones exposed were met with a terrible fever and cough. When the ill would cough, more of the fog would come bursting out of their lungs. This strange phenomenon accelerated the spread of the fog and very soon, no city was left untouched.

The population was devastated. Many people died of the illness. Governments broke, communities fractured, families destroyed. Social order fell apart and society crumbled.

Not everyone who was infected by the fog perished. Some made it through the ordeal. Even more curious, there were people who were completely immune to the disease.  
Fifteen years have passed. The survivors have since coalesced into smaller communities throughout the world. Banding together to create their own little worlds of comfort.

So the world has gone through some kind of disaster. Typical social order has fallen apart and many people have died. The people who survived decided to band together and create small communities.

  

Though much of the landscape is barren, most of these communities have managed to achieve some amount of comfort. I would imagine the state of technology in this world is about the same as the real world, but perhaps some goods are more scarce and a lot of technology has a sort of patchwork quality to it.

  

As the years passed, some passionate fellows decided to establish a guild of postal carriers. They believe in the importance of having a working mail system as they believe it helps to bring humanity close together (in the new world, most communications technologies, like cell phones and internet, probably wouldn't work very well or not at all)

  

As such, this guild has worked to create numerous chapters around the world. They coordinate with each other to create a world wide network.

  
  
  

Postal Carriers guild:

  

The postal carrier guild was established by people who were postal carriers in the old world before the disaster. Most of the older workers are still active (as it has only been fifteen years) and they generally serve in higher positions. Most chapter leaders are older people who helped to establish the guild.

  

Our main character is in her early twenties and has recently finished her training to become a postal carrier. She is very eager and enthusiastic about her job and very much looks forward to all of the new people she is going to meet.

  

Due to the nature of this new world, postal carriers are expected to be a bit more hardy than they used to be. Because they may travel between towns/cities and because so much of the wilderness has taken over, they need to be equipped with certain survival skills.
