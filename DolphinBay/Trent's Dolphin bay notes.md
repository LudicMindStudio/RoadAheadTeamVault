# Synopsis

Dolphin bay is a town that should be known for one thing: Dolphins. But in recent times, dolphins are a rare sight for many of its residents. The reason for this is highly connected to the fog and the group think of the citizens of dolphin bay. This story is about the snowballing of negativity and the ease of falling into dogmatic group think.

The creation of dolphin bay came with the forming of the Dolphin Sanctuary, a building, initiative, and promise to help the dolphins native to the area. The dolphin sanctuary always stayed in the hearts of the citizens and is part of the dolphin bay identity. But with time as the town grew and infrastructure concerns for other facets of life developed, not everyone in dolphin bay always had time for the dolphins. With a few full time staffed dolphin sanctuary workers, the rest of the town had other day to day concerns. 

This leads to the recent conflict of the region, less dolphins are appearing and blame is being thrown around. The reason for the initial decrease in dolphins is ambiguous. It could be the start of the fog pushing some of the dolphins out, a natural occurrence, or any other simple reason. Regardless of how it started, the stories main perpetrator **Francois Hustle** notices the lack of dolphins and wants to make a change. But through a turn of events, he ends up only amplifying the issue

Francois can't find one of the dolphins that hes close to an goes to take a visit to the dolphin sanctuary for help. They are unable to help him, but communication gets heated and Francois uses his emotions to place blame. It is after this event that Francois begins his campaign to nitpick, scrutinise, and escalate the problems effecting Dolphin bay.

Francois uses his skills in public speaking to bring to light the issue of diminishing dolphins to the people of dolphin bay. He gains supporters, and while he is peaceful in his protesting actions, the scentement that he fosters in his rallies are of fear, concern, and mistrust. He leads the town into believing the news about the dolphins and causes mistrust of the sanctuary and its workers. The overall effect on dolphin bay is a shattered community and gossipy, doom ridden people. 

A key part to this story is the real escalation in Francois' actions. Due to the world and the fog, the negative emotions that Francois is causing has real snowballing effects. With the added negativity, the fog rolls in closer and closer to the town, the negative and compromised mental health of the citizens feeding its growth. With more fog less dolphins are being seen, more dolphins are leaving, and Francois has more fuel to further his initiative.  

This bleak state is where Lakshmi enters. Flipping the script and inverting the snowball is the ultimate resolution of the story and what Lakshmi needs to discover. At first, this isn't really Lakshmi's business and the dots wont connect on what to do. She has to discover the reality of things, form a plan of recovery, then convince the town to follow her lead and start the healing process.

Some key features on Lakshmi's involvement will be kids. The kids of dolphin bay are completely unaffected by Francois' movement and are able to help teach Lakshmi the connection between the fog, emotions, and animals. Lakshmi also can learn about Francois' backstory and origins for his actions. To de-vilianify everyone in the situation is a requirement for progress. Finally Lakshmi should also need to bolster her own confidence and find the courage to make a change at the level of effecting the whole city. Other events and characters around the city help her do this.

By the end of the story, everyone makes peace and is on a road ahead to reverting the damages done and making dolphin bay the best it can be. Dolphins begin to return and progress can be seen with more dolphins and other positive activist actions. The activists and the dolphin sanctuary work together, each using their skills in their own ways to help the dolphins.

# Characters

**Francois Hustle**
Francois hustle really loves dolphins. why would he start such a fuss if he didn't? In fact Hustle had a very close relationship with one of the dolphins that he named Fin. Although the dolphins were welcome in dolphin bay sanctuary, Fin decides not join his kin as much and has other hideouts around dolphin bay. This is where he met with Hustle, and the two having similar out group personalities, they formed a bond.
