		- The mail is a lab tool for the research she's doing
		- [...]
	- Event 3: Lakshmi goes to deliver mail to a [relatively] young man
		- He isn't home, so she just drops off the mail at his door
	- Event 4: The next day, she goes back to see Greg for the mail to deliver
		- On her way out she sees the intern being all lost once again and she goes to interact with him
		- They talk, he tells her that he's supposed to deliver this package but is too shy and nervous to go on his own
		- Lakshmi tells him to come with him and they'll do delivery together for the day (it's a chill day she also only has one package to deliver)

		- They go to deliver her mail

		- The package she delivered in still in front of the door.
		- They drop his new package and go back to the office

		- At the office, Lakshmi goes to tell Greg about the man who either doesn't live at that address anymore, or isn't home.
		- He tells her that their job is only to deliver mail, not to play detectives, but you can see that Lakshmi isn't quite satisfied with that answer and doesn't want to let things go

		- After delivering all the mail (the player won't be delivering that day (he got the point)), the game loads to the next, at sunset, in front of the man's house. His packages are still untouched at the front door

		- Despite Olavius' opposition of entering into a stranger's home, Lakshmi still finds a way through a window

		- They search around the house, hoping to find some kind of clue as to where the man went.

		- Lakshmi reads the letter, where the disappeared man talks about why there are no more dolphins around the bay. What is the piece of history that's been erased? Does it have anything to do with the Fog? What is the secret of the dolphins?

		- Once she finishes reading, she hears a scream from Olavius behind her who passes out after he says "THE HOMEOWNER! HE'S HERE!"
