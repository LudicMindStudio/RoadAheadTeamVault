Top down map making can be hard. 
We always need to consider the angle at which our camera is supposed to be, while not perfectly top down top down (we dont only see the tops of character heads; not birds eye) we also arent making an isometric game. 
We also need to always consider depth. Layers of height should still feel significant and with a sense of scale

Thanksfully there are plenty of other games we can use as inspiration!
