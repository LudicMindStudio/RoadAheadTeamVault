So this is the chalet set up. The size of the scene can of course be extended as desired, for example the fences, dirt road, cliff side, can be extended or shortened. 

The trees follow a simple pattern, one is placed more inwards and the other outwards to create a sense of fullness. (though you can alter them as you please)

the grass blades, flowers and rocks can be arranged however you want, I placed them in random areas but feel free to switch it up.
And you'll notice they have shadowy textures underneath, I made extra sprites for those which are posted under assets! feel free to use or not use them lol (they can be used as shadows- placed under something or just grass textures of their own)

Below the cliff, the grass transitioning into sand is not a tile set we have, I used an edge tile from my grass tile set and just drew in the sand next to it. Of course, feel free to leave that part out, or let me know if you need a transitioning tile. 

If you have any questions, please let me know!
