## The story format


```
dolphin bay
  the main story
    acts
      quests
        scenes
          events / interactables / dialogues
```

- each level is a file, started from a template, living inside its parent
- characters, locations, flags, mail and the journal attach at every level
- act 1 is the working example. every act after it follows the same tree
- the full glossary is the [[Writer's Index]]. the tree drawn out is the [[Writer Document Hierarchy]]

---

## The words we use

- act: a big turn in the story. we run four
- quest: a job the player takes on, with a start and a clear
- scene: the core action of a quest, set in a location
- event: something that happens when the player enters a place
- interactable: something the player chooses to touch or read
- dialogue: the exchange itself, chains with options
- poi: a story spot inside a location. the market, the podium, the bench
- flag: a yes/no the game remembers. did she visit the hall
- journal: the players quest log. one doc per quest

## Themes and player lessons

- the themes this story carries: psychosis experiences, assertive communication, active listening skills, reality grounding, community ties, acceptance
- lakshmis arc: courage, confidence, and a coping strategy she gains along the way
- the player sees the snowball first: fear and group think feed the fog, and the fog feeds them right back
- then the player sees it invert: connection, listening, and community are how lakshmi rolls it back
- you dont fix people. you understand them, and that gives them room to heal

## The four acts

- act 1: graduation, with quiet unease underneath. focus stays on the ceremony. hints for players who look
- act 2: the town, the protests, the people. unrest a curious player can investigate. ends as lakshmi walks into the worst of it
- act 3: the search. what happened with francois, what happened with the sanctuary, what the town needs to hear. lakshmi figures out who to tell what, with the fog revealing the key. the act ends with her choice to act
- act 4: the confrontation and the thaw. francois reached as a person, the town reached as a crowd

## The story is interwoven into the gameplay

- mail is the core loop of the game. the story rides on it
- the loop: the mail room -> find them on their day -> deliver -> they react -> the day turns
- every delivery serves story gameplay in many ways:
	- the player navigates around the map, allowing them to stumble into events, or other npcs to talk to
	- mail explictly leading the player somewhere
	- a mail item being explictly for someone about something (key mail)
- mail is a clock. when we are ready to move on, the relvent mail appears

---

## Canon scenes and texture

- most content is the town living. deliveries, small talk, routine. the story is felt in the community, picked up on in pieces by the player
- canon scenes are the ones the story cant happen without. they get named, owned, and polished hardest

---

## Storytelling through the medium of games

- the player takes the steps. the story is what happens on the way
- strongest to quietest: gameplay -> scenes -> events -> dialogue. show, dont tell
- progress needs to feel earned, by the player doing or thinking something
- routine is the drumbeat. days repeat, so when something changes it gets loud
- Think: how is this character acting based on whats going on?
