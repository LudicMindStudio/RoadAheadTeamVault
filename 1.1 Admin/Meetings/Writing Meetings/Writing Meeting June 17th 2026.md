# Live Notes

## Consensus

- **Fewer NPCs, more depth.** Directional aim: ~15-20 total, ~5 with full character stories. "Less NPCs, more room for polish."
- **Characters first.** Build NPCs → locations → story emerges naturally. This is the workstream sequence.
- **Essential reactions for every NPC.** Mail delivery dialogue is non-negotiable. Misc reactions (events, time-of-day, situational) are optional, per-writer interest.
- **Inter-NPC relationships matter.** What does each character think of every other? Not dialogue per pair — just awareness. Corina already started a relationship map.
- **Behaviours can be loose.** In Obsidian: "they like this spot, they do this at night." Formal ActionGraph schedules come during implementation.
- **Mail items are flexible.** Letters, packages, balloons, whatever fits the character. Art production is the constraint, not system design.
- **Factions as an organizing layer.** Kids, guild members, protesters, etc. Worth its own doc type. Helps categorize NPCs and relationships.
- **Memories are a separate workstream.** Not the writing team's focus atm.
