---

```

July — Characters (profiles, dialogue, sprites, relationships)
```

## Who does what

- **Writers + Artists** - you make the content. characters, dialogue, art, locations
  - a lot of independent creative work
  - everything else templates exist for everything. just gotta fill em in
- **Trent** - engine, tools, majority implementation
  - by end of summer you'll have the full toolset in unity
  - but we're not waiting for that. writing and art starts now
  - other implementation efforts will be shown off before then

---

## Build order

- **Characters first.** who lives here? what do they want?
- **Then locations.** where do they live? what does the town look like?
- **Then story.** now that we know the people and the place — what happens?

- If we write the story before we know the characters, it won't feel real
- if we build the characters first, the story writes itself
