## Recall why we are doing sprints
- Sprints should atomize the creative process
- We go from this meeting, starting with one goal, and see where we end up
- Once done, we tie a little bow on it, no matter how messy, even if we're going to tear off the wrapping paper immediately next sprint
- Sprints are to learn from, what we did well and how to do better
---
## Goal of this sprint 
- Finish and polish what’s already in motion
- Keep ourselves accountable and communicate  
- If we end this sprint with slightly less content but way more clarity, that’s a win
---
## Loose Ideas
- If I gave no direction what would you do next?
- Lets tread well our path, and look at our decisions
- Under what conditions does a task come to you? Under what conditions do you leave it for the next person?
- Your creative nook should be well defined

## Specifics
- Sprint Name: **The Foundation Sprint**
- By the end we will have the foundation of our game, and the foundation of our team
- We are setting a standard for ourselves in the future
- Artist style unified, Writing voice is clear, Engine is understandable
- We end with a game still in a fledgling state that we all understand. We know our own speciality and how to help it
