## Where We Are

- Character art remains the most impactful.
- Locations should be given some identity.
- The artists get the opportunity to design to their strengths and liking

---
### House Exteriors

- Proposal: unique sprite per house. One household accent carries the identity.
	- Sammy's turtle mailbox at Garp's. Gatz's art yard plus Proudhon the fish. Shirley's garden beds at Doran & Shirley.
- One accent reads instantly and stays cheap. No full re-architecture per house.
---
### Interiors & Key Items

- Interiors share a tileset base for the demo, not fully unique.
- Personality comes from key items.
	- Key items will be used as see fit. In mail, stoies, and in the world.
	- New Key items will appear per household

---
### The Map Asset

- Based off the Map sketches
