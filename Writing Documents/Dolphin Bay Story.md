# Act1 - Awaken Lakshmi

## Characters

**[[Lakshmi]]**
Other then in the prologue, the player has no idea about anything. Lakshmi should be as blank as she has can be.

Her tone is optimistic but experience-less. She asks a lot of questions but when she thinks she knows something, shes confident about it. She's empathetic but unable to correctly express herself. Lakshmi doesn't innately seem to have any drive other then to graduate and become a postal worker. As a youth, she is a blank slate.


**Grandpa Dan**
Grandpa Dan is introduced as comedic more then anything. his bumbling in his role as Lakshmi's model should be obvious. As a former guild member and self serious person, he has a strong ego that seeps into all of his dialogue. He deeply cares about Lakshmi though and uses his free time as a retiree often for her. Grandpa Dan can be seen supporting Lakshmi in semi circuitous ways to reflect these conflicting feeling at this point

With the graduation of Lakshmi approaching, Grandpa Dan knows that soon he will have less time with her. His dwindling time to be sincere with Lakshmi is boiling into his character and usual actions until the moment she leaves.

**Greg**
Greg is the more obvious model for Lakshmi in this moment. As the leader of the postal guild, he acts as the goal Lakshmi is chasing. Also in contrast to Grandpa, Greg is much more prepared for Lakshmi's graduation. His high hopes for Lakshmi stray close to expectations as he continues to guide Lakshmi in her early steps as a guild member. At this point in the story Greg is perfect, a guide who has all the answers.

**Francois Hustle**
Francois is at his worst. He is fully being pulled around by his psychosis in a destructive way. He has seen the "success" in raising the numbers of his movement and sees the decline of the dolphins as more fuel for his cause. He's internalised the dogmatic thinking that the sanctuary is the villain. He's actively trying to spread his negative and destructive mindset and can openly be seen doing so.

**Scene 1 - Wake up Lakshmi**
### Synopsis

This is the very first scene that takes place in the game (other then prologue)

The scene starts with Lakshmi waking up from bed late. She reminisces about strange dreams and gives a short but identifying statement about her current self. Lakshmi recalls that there was something important Greg reminded her the previous day and she sets her goal to getting to the guildhall asap. She prepares herself for a bit and the player can explore the upstairs of the house. 

When Lakshmi goes downstairs she catches Grandpa Dan leaving her a hearty breakfast. Grandpa Dan admits to making Lakshmi breakfast and also informs her to get to the Guild ASAP today. The player can be offered additional dialogue here with Grandpa both comedic and informative. Once Lakshmi is done talking grandpa dan storms out of the house. 

Outside of the house Lakshmi can see Grandpa leaving and making his way back to the town, but Lakshmi can also notice some sounds of a crowd  coming from beyond the wood line, notability towards the sanctuary.

**Scene 2.1 - The practice mail**
### Synopsis
 This scene serves as an introduction to the mail delivery mechanics, along with an introduction to Lakshmi's memory entry powers. This scene also introduces Quinn/Olavius/The Intern. The initial portrayal of The intern is flat and one dimensional as someone with low self esteem and an easy outlet for Lakshmi's high energy.

The scene starts with Greg explaining the second part of the graduation test, a practical. Specifically he explains that the new Intern has the day off today but needs something delivered. Lakshmi then goes to the counter and the player is given general mail delivery tutorials. The player has to use the environment to figure out where to go.

Once they arrive at the Interns house, they are called to come in and notice the Intern is actually bed ridden. Lakshmi expresses her surprise and offers to help the Intern. The Intern stays humble and lets Lakshmi know that that's what the package is for, its medicine. Lakshmi almost forgets to keep her composure and successfully follows guild procedures in packing and verifying the mail delivery. Its important to quickly establish in the player (and Lakshmi) pity and empathy for the intern through some quick dialogue between the two.

Once lakshmi delivers the package she offers to help administer the medicine.  She has some internal dialogue reflecting on the scenario, her relationship to the intern, and her beliefs (player choice). Then as the medicine is being administered Lakshmi studders and begins to enter one of the Interns memories. Since this is the first scene with Lakshmi entering a memory it should be played up a little.
